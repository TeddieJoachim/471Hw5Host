2. Discuss the voltage level at the new node 6 correctly. (-3)

     The nodal voltages are the sum the voltage drops over the three edges in
     a loop between 0, 6, and 1.  And this sum must be 0.  Because of the
     forcing "s" the voltages at nodes 0 and 1 are unlikely to change a lot,
     so the new node will have to equalize the voltage between them.  The
     previous voltage values for 0 and 1 were 0.5325835 and -0.35436157
     respectively, so then if the voltages don't change much, the value at 6
     will be around (0.5325835  -0.35436157) / 2 = 0.08911.  This is indeed
     close to what we see.  The new voltages, after node 6 is added, at nodes
     0 and 1 are 0.33094 and -0.2363, respectively.  And the voltage at node 6
     is then (0.33094 - 0.2363) / 2 = 0.04732
